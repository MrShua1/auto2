# Pre-Generation Lint

Block image generation until every shot passes.

## Editorial

- [ ] Every explicit user-supplied shot direction is copied into a lock with its original sequence position.
- [ ] Subject, scale, viewpoint, angle, screen direction and action instant match all locked fields exactly.
- [ ] Automated coverage filled only unspecified fields; it did not merge, delete, reorder, substitute or re-angle a locked shot.
- [ ] Any physical conflict between locked directions is named explicitly and generation remains blocked pending one decisive resolution.
- [ ] One unique narrative/editorial job.
- [ ] Coverage type declared: detail, establish, subjective, performance/reaction, afterimage/transition.
- [ ] Cut-in and cut-out relationships are concrete.
- [ ] Removing the shot would lose information, pressure, relationship, causality or transition.
- [ ] Scale/angle differs from adjacent shots for a reason, or intentional match is documented.

## Camera

- [ ] Shot scale is exact.
- [ ] Focal length/lens behavior is exact.
- [ ] Aperture or depth behavior is exact.
- [ ] Camera height and angle are exact.
- [ ] Composition and visual center are exact.
- [ ] Subject placement and screen direction are exact.
- [ ] Future camera movement has one primary behavior, speed and endpoint, or is locked.
- [ ] One primary depth mechanism; no redundant fog/blur/diffusion stack.

## State And Motion

- [ ] One frozen instant only.
- [ ] Actor/object and action threshold are named.
- [ ] Force level and physical consequence are plausible when applicable.
- [ ] Endpoint/handoff is visible.
- [ ] Motion blur is limited to the moving object.

## Character And Wardrobe

- [ ] Identity authority assigned per named character.
- [ ] Storyboard/continuity references cannot overwrite identity.
- [ ] Left/right, foreground/background and pose are unambiguous.
- [ ] Upper garment, lower garment, legwear, footwear and bag/accessories are stated.
- [ ] Forbidden source garments are named.
- [ ] Exact visible people and fragile anatomy counts are stated where needed.
- [ ] Expression and gaze are observable, not abstract emotion labels.

## Lighting And LOOK

- [ ] Physical key source, direction and color temperature.
- [ ] Fill level and shadow behavior.
- [ ] Reflection behavior for wet/metal/glass surfaces.
- [ ] Atmosphere has a real medium and motivated source, otherwise omitted.
- [ ] LOOK ID is stable across the sequence.
- [ ] Skin, tonal curve, texture scale and realism are stable.
- [ ] Scene temperature variations are physically motivated.

## Anti-Slop And Rights

- [ ] `cinematic`, `beautiful`, `epic`, `dynamic`, `professional` are translated into production facts.
- [ ] No generic giant negative list; model-aware prose constraints only.
- [ ] No waxy skin, CGI sheen, digital oversharpening, unsupported haze or flat global fill.
- [ ] Named studio/film/DP shorthand is translated into observable descriptors.
- [ ] No shot-for-shot recreation, logos, protected dialogue or unauthorized likeness inference.

## Delivery

- [ ] Independent true-16:9 master requested.
- [ ] Exact reference order and role recorded.
- [ ] Credentials and endpoint fields remain outside creative prose/logs.
- [ ] Contact sheet waits for master approval and uses proportional deterministic assembly.

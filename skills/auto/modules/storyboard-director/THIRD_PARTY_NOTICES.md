# Third-Party Interoperability

This Skill orchestrates independently installed Skills through responsibility
boundaries. It does not copy or relicense their full rule text.

- `cinematic-prompt-engine`: AI Cinematic Prompt by Leo414x,
  <https://github.com/Leo414x/AI_Cinematic_Prompt>, restricted license. Preserve
  its author, upstream and `LICENSE`; commercial use may require written permission.
- `seedance-20` specialist Skills: copyright and license remain in their package.
- `ai-short-film-director` and `gpt-10s-nine-grid-storyboard`: use their package
  licenses and notices.
- `cinematic-director`: Wangzhang Wu's DirectorSKILL,
  <https://github.com/wuwangzhang1216/DirectorSKILL>, MIT license, pinned and
  locally patched as documented in its `UPSTREAM.md`.

The orchestration, contract schema and lint checklist in this directory are an
original interoperability layer distributed under MIT.

# EDL, Pickups, Audio Post, And Delivery

## Picture Assembly

Build the edit only from accepted takes. The EDL should record source, source in/out, timeline in/out, transition, audio treatment, and boundary risk.

At every boundary, classify:

- Clean cut.
- Match action.
- Eyeline or screen-direction cut.
- Sound bridge/J-cut/L-cut.
- Dissolve or time transition.
- Pickup required.

Do not regenerate a whole scene when a 1-2 second bridge, crop, speed adjustment, or reaction pickup can repair the edit. If the endpoint minimum is 4 seconds, generate at least 4 seconds and use only the required 1-2 seconds in the EDL.

## Pickup Queue

Each pickup must state:

- Broken boundary or missing story information.
- Minimum required duration.
- Canonical references.
- Start/end state.
- Whether dialogue is prohibited.
- Where it inserts in the EDL.
- Maximum attempt count.

Pickups are production clips and must pass the same prompt lint and take review.

## Audio Post

After picture lock:

1. Rebuild the audio map from actual timeline timecodes.
2. Keep usable generated dialogue and sync-critical SFX.
3. Add room tone and ambience continuity across cuts.
4. Compose/select one score arc against the locked timeline.
5. Mix dialogue, ambience, SFX, and score by narrative priority.
6. Add subtitles and on-screen text in post.
7. Check loudness and head/tail silence for the delivery platform.

Do not assume independently generated music will remain continuous across clips.

## Release QA

Block release on:

- Wrong total duration or aspect.
- Missing/duplicate beat.
- Character, wardrobe, prop, location, time, or screen-direction break.
- Visible text artifacts, logos, watermarks, or unsafe copyrighted material.
- Dialogue/subtitle mismatch.
- Audio pop, abrupt ambience, clipping, or score discontinuity.
- Unresolved placeholder, unreviewed take, or missing attribution/rights record.

Record final technical settings, accepted source list, rights notes, and known limitations.
